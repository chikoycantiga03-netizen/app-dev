## My Favorite Movie/Series: Ang Probinsyano

### About
**Ang Probinsyano** is a Filipino action-drama about a good-hearted police officer who fights corruption and protects the people he loves. It started as a *1997 movie* starring **Fernando Poe Jr.** and was later made into a long-running **ABS-CBN TV series** starring **Coco Martin**.

> A story about courage, family, and standing up for what is right.

### Main Characters
1. Ricardo "Cardo" Dalisay
2. Alyana Arevalo
3. Lola Flora
4. Joaquin Tuazon

### Why I Like It
- Exciting action scenes
- Strong themes about *family* and **justice**
  - Loyalty to loved ones
  - Fighting corruption
- A story many Filipinos can relate to
